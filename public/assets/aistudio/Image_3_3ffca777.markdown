## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a cinematic culinary atmosphere: the tension between primitive open-flame cooking and refined contemporary gastronomy. The visual direction balances the quiet restraint of high-end editorial publishing with visceral warmth, drawing on textured shadows, amber glow, and architectural balance.

### Creative Philosophy
- **Atmospheric Chiaroscuro:** Deep espresso and charred wood tones serve as the canvas, punctuated by embers of terracotta and brass. Negative space is treated as physical shadow, lending depth and presence to imagery.
- **Editorial Poise:** Layouts balance asymmetry, wide margins, and refined typographic pacing. Text alternates between high-contrast display serif typography and precise geometric sans-serif details.
- **Sensory Tactility:** Visual elements evoke craft and texture—hairline rules, muted metallic accents, and fluid interactions mimicking heat, smoke, and flame.

### Target Audience & Tone
Designed for discerning diners, culinary travelers, and design enthusiasts who value provenance, craftsmanship, and ambiance. The experience feels welcoming, confident, deliberate, and immersive.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the kitchen hearth: charred embers, glowing terracotta, aged oak, and cast iron.

### Core Color Roles
- **Primary (`#C86446` - Terracotta Ember):** Represents active flame and hearth heat. Used for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, and critical highlights.
- **Secondary (`#D4A373` - Burnished Brass):** Evokes interior brass fixtures and aged oak. Used for secondary interactive moments, badges, and decorative accents.
- **Tertiary (`#B35438` - Smoldering Rust):** Deep heat tone utilized for hover states, focus outlines, and urgent states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#1A1412` - Deep Espresso):** The primary base tone across canvas and broad backdrops. Provides depth while maintaining more warmth than pure black.

### Surface Hierarchy & Supporting Tones
- **Canvas Base:** `#120D0B` (Cast Iron / Charred Hearth)
- **Surface Elevation 1:** `#1A1412` (Deep Espresso)
- **Surface Elevation 2:** `#221F1E` (Warm Charcoal)
- **Surface Elevation 3:** `#2D2825` (Aged Bark)
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - **High-Contrast Text:** `#FAF6F0` (Warm Ivory)
  - **Muted Text:** `#D9D1C7` (Soft Linen)
  - **Subtle / Caption Text:** `#9E958C` (Ash Grey)
- **Structural Lines:** `rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.08)` (Ivory Hairline)

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic craft and contemporary refinement. Headings leverage Playfair Display's classical, high-contrast strokes and elegant italics, while Plus Jakarta Sans brings legibility, openness, and architectural structure to menus, details, and forms.

### Usage Directives
- **Italic Inflections:** Headings can accent specific words in italic styles (e.g., *"Hearth-Smoked"* or *"Provenance"*) to reinforce editorial rhythm.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows:** Eyebrow labels, category names, dish tags, and navigation links use `label-caps` in uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.18em`).
- **Editorial Pairings:** Pair display headlines directly over `body-lg` introductory paragraphs with balanced spacing (`space-md`) to maintain editorial cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid, asymmetric 12-column grid system built around deliberate breathing room, generous pacing, and editorial alignment.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `4rem` outer margin, `2rem` gutter. Maximum content bounds cap at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `2.5rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutter.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column grid, `1.25rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutter.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Rhythmic Negative Space:** Section transitions utilize deep vertical padding (`5rem` to `8rem` on desktop) to evoke quiet luxury and prevent information density.
- **Asymmetrical Photo Pairings:** Imagery combines full-bleed moments with offset 7:5 and 5:7 aspect ratios, allowing photography of hearth, smoke, and plating to step across vertical grid axes.
- **Hairline Boundary Grid:** Sections are separated by subtle ivory hairlines (`rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.08)`), replacing heavy container cards with architectural framing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal planes, diffused warm lighting, and subtle outlines rather than standard dropshadows.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Tonal Tiers:** Surfaces rise in value as they layer upward. Canvas background sits at `#120D0B`, overlay cards and panels rest at `#1A1412`, and floating elements (modals, drawers) elevate to `#221F1E`.
- **Hearth Glow Effects:** Active elements, highlighted reservation blocks, and focal points utilize warm radial luminance (`rgba(200, 100, 70, 0.12)` spread over 48px) rather than harsh drop shadows.
- **Hairline Framing:** Floating panels and interactive modules feature a 1px perimeter outline (`rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.08)`), defining boundaries against dark backgrounds.
- **Backdrop Diffusion:** Modal scrims and sticky navigation bars apply a 16px to 24px backdrop blur over `rgba(18, 13, 11, 0.85)` for seamless legibility over dynamic food photography.

## Shapes

The design system employs an architectural, sharp shape language (`0px` border radius) across foundational elements, complemented by select pill geometry strictly reserved for discrete tag chips.

### Form Rationale
- **Razor Precision:** Buttons, cards, form inputs, dialogs, and photographic frames adhere strictly to clean `0px` corners, reflecting iron plateware, marble counters, and architectural integrity.
- **Contrasting Badges:** Discrete dietary chips, tasting menu labels, or status indicators utilize circular pills (`rounded-full`) to deliver contrast against the underlying rectangular framework.
- **Photographic Insets:** Images follow crisp geometric borders, accented by offset hairline frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid terracotta (`#C86446`) with ivory text (`#FAF6F0`), uppercase `label-caps` typography, `0px` radius, padded at `1rem 2rem`. On hover, background shifts to `#B35438` with an amber glow.
- **Secondary / Brass Outline:** Transparent surface with a 1px brass hairline border (`#D4A373`), ivory text, and smooth brass fill on hover (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.1)`).
- **Text Action:** Playfair Display italic text with a persistent bottom border resting 4px below baseline; expands slightly on hover.

### Form Inputs & Reservation Flow
- **Fields:** Flush dark backgrounds (`#120D0B`) with subtle bottom borders (`rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.2)`). No enclosing card box; inputs resemble a handwritten ledger.
- **Active / Focus:** Bottom border elevates to terracotta (`#C86446`) with an upward floating label in `label-caps` styling (`#D4A373`).
- **Date & Party Size Selectors:** Tabular horizontal grid of sharp-corner tiles with subtle border dividers. Active selection fills with `#221F1E` and is framed in `#C86446`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Dietary & Tasting Flags:** Pill-shaped with subtle hairline borders (`rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.15)`), tinted background (`rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.03)`), and `label-caps` text in `#D9D1C7`.

### Lists & Culinary Menus
- **Dish Row:** 2-column or full-width row with dish name in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, dotted or hairline trailing leader to price in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, followed by ingredients in `body-sm` (`#9E958C`).
- **Separators:** 1px hairline rules (`rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.08)`) with `space-md` vertical padding.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Design:** Custom minimalist squares and circles with a 1px hairline border (`#9E958C`). On check, inner marker reveals an ember terracotta fill (`#C86446`).

### Cards & Story Panels
- **Editorial Cards:** Asymmetrical imagery paired with text below, separated by hairline rules. Avoid elevated drop shadow boxes; let content rest directly on the `#1A1412` canvas.
